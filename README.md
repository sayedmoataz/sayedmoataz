### 👨‍💻 About Me

I build Flutter and native mobile apps for **banking, fintech, e-commerce, real estate, education and AI**, with **4+ years** of production experience. I'm currently architecting an enterprise **mobile banking platform** at **Cubic Information Systems**.

- 🛡️ **Security & compliance**: OWASP Mobile Top 10 controls, SSL pinning and tamper detection on a tier-1 banking platform, verified by third-party penetration tests.
- ⚡ **Performance**: cut a 30-second cold start to under one second with lazy initialization, deferred background work and parallel loading.
- 💸 **Cost & network efficiency**: brought Google Maps spend from \$3,500 to under \$500 a month and removed 60% of redundant network calls.
- 🤖 **AI integration**: OpenAI Assistants, vector search and GraphRAG pipelines with 95% query precision, plus low-latency voice interfaces.
- 🧩 **Native + cross-platform**: Flutter at the core, with custom platform channels in Kotlin and Swift, and Kotlin Multiplatform where it fits.
- 🧑‍🏫 **Quality & leadership**: strict TDD with bloc tests and Mocktail, on-site UAT with clients, and mentoring interns.
- 🎓 B.Sc. in Computer Science, **Helwan University (FCAI)**.
